How I measured this
  • I estimated currents with a power model built from the firmware's own once-a-second logs. The board ran on USB with no battery.
  • I ran the same 18-minute scripted stress test every time, from dim idle to everything at once. A round passed only if power dropped and nothing you'd notice got slower.
  • I compared each round with the one before it, run at the same hour of the night. Battery life and the dim screen chain those comparisons back to the baseline.
  • The full details are in docs/power/summary.md and docs/power/log.md in my repository.
